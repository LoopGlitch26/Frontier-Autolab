# World Briefing: Era E9 (projected, as of January 2040)
*Prepared by The Auditor. **This is a projected base case, not reality.** It extrapolates from real trends to Sep 2026, the E8 briefing, and the E8 audit. Tags: **FACT(2026)** = public by Sep 2026; **EXTRAPOLATION** = a trend carried forward; **SPECULATION** = a scenario judgment. Confidence is H/M/L. Several items cut against the Clearwork thesis on purpose. Uncertainty over 14 years is wide: treat every non-FACT line as one draw from a distribution.*

## Pace of capability: three live branches
- **FACT(2026), H:** METR horizons ≥16h (Mythos Preview), monthly frontier releases, and hyperscaler capex of ~$650–730B/yr.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M (base, ~55%):** progress continued but slowed further after ~2034. Power, grid interconnects and costly real-world verification bound it, not algorithms. Frontier agents run quarter-long projects inside firms. "Autonomous work-months" replaced the work-week metric, and scientific-discovery agents are routine in materials and drug design.
- **SPECULATION, L-M (slowdown, ~25%):** a 2033–35 capex bust (drawdowns above 50%) and diminishing returns flattened the frontier. AI is embedded everywhere but mostly as mature, cheap, open-weight infrastructure. Value moved to deployers and incumbents with distribution.
- **SPECULATION, L (radical acceleration, ~20%):** credible automated AI research began around 2035. A small number of lab-state compacts control frontier systems, and the enterprise "agent market" is largely a set of internal products of 2–3 entities. Most layer theses from earlier eras are moot in this branch.

## Delegated authority and settlement (against the thesis)
- **FACT(2026), H:** AP2 (Google), ACP (OpenAI/Stripe), Visa Intelligent Commerce, Mastercard Agent Pay and Entra Agent ID define agent mandates and identities.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M-H:** payment networks and lab platforms absorbed most settlement. "Conditional release" and outcome holdbacks are standard features of agent-payment mandates, priced inside interchange or platform fees. Cross-vendor delayed-outcome settlement exists but is a mid-sized, standards-and-data niche, not a franchise.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** disputes over agent work are resolved mostly through **warranties and claims** rather than escrow. Vendors carry bundled E&O, and carriers adjudicate on OTel-style traces plus mandate records.
- **SPECULATION, M:** one "DTCC for agent work" utility exists in one jurisdiction (most likely the EU financial sector), co-owned by banks and carriers after a correlated-loss event.

## Law and liability: human sign-off erodes
- **FACT(2026), H:** EU AI Act high-risk duties are scheduled for Dec 2027/Aug 2028; Moffatt v. Air Canada rejects "the AI did it."
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** **human sign-off is no longer legally required** for most low- and medium-risk agent work in the EU, UK and much of the US. Safe harbors let certified agents with insured deployers act without per-task human acceptance. Liability attaches to the deployer and its insurer, not to a named reviewer. Sign-off survives in medicine, criminal justice, large credit decisions and critical infrastructure.
- **SPECULATION, L-M:** limited legal personhood or "bonded agent" status exists in at least one jurisdiction: an agent posts a bond or insurance and can contract within limits.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** agent liability cover is a large line (tens of billions in premium, L on size), dominated by existing carriers and 1–2 specialist modelers. Systemic-risk sublimits are standard.

## Compute, energy, geopolitics
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** power is the binding constraint. Nuclear restarts, SMR pilots, gas and huge solar-plus-storage builds feed data centers, and the politics of electricity prices is heated.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** US–China AI competition persists. Open-weight models from China and elsewhere are 6–18 months behind and dominate cost-sensitive and Global South deployment. Frontier-weight security is treated like dual-use defense material.
- **SPECULATION, L-M:** at least one serious AI-enabled cyber or biosecurity incident led to binding international compute or access controls.

## Physical world
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** robots are common in warehouses, logistics, manufacturing and agriculture, and early humanoid deployment is under way in commercial settings. Home robots are a premium niche. Fleet owners hold the embodied data moat.
- **SPECULATION, L:** autonomous labs and robot-built infrastructure are being tested for lunar or orbital use. Crewed Mars presence is not established.

## Economy and society
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** routine cognitive work is largely automated. Entry-level professional pathways have been rebuilt around apprenticeship with agents. Labor share has fallen, and AI and work is the top political issue in several democracies.
- **SPECULATION, L-M:** compute dividends, AI-funded income supplements or shortened work weeks are national policy in 1–3 OECD countries.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** software costs dropped close to zero for many categories. Margins pooled in energy, compute, distribution, regulated licenses, liability-bearing balance sheets and proprietary real-world data.

## Leading indicators to check at era start
Which pace branch we are in (frontier release cadence, lab revenue growth, capex trend); share of agent payments settled with conditional release via networks versus third parties; whether safe harbors removed human sign-off in finance; size and concentration of the agent-liability premium pool; any correlated-loss event and the regulatory response; open-weight lag.
