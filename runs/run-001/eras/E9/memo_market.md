## DEPARTMENT RECOMMENDATION

### Thesis A (preferred): Clearwork Bond, an agent-liability MGA and bonded-agent program
Capability: certified agents act on quarter-long projects without per-task human sign-off (EXTRAPOLATION, M) → Adoption: safe harbors move liability to deployers and insurers, and the agent-liability line is tens of billions in premium (EXTRAPOLATION, M; L on size) → Bottleneck: pricing and settling claims on autonomous work with correlated tails. → **Layer we own:** delegated underwriting authority plus claims adjudication, priced on our eight years of mandate × consequence history, with capacity from 2 carriers and a reinsurer sidecar → Data: our own loss triangles, which no licensee can buy from us → Next capability: "authority limits" as a credential, meaning an agent's bond size and scope become its credit rating. Bonded-agent status, where one exists, is the upside (SPECULATION, L-M).
- **Why now:** sign-off removal takes out the human control that used to cap losses. Systemic sublimits leave most of the premium pool mispriced (EXTRAPOLATION, M).
- **Wedge:** a bonded program for agent vendors in mid-market finance operations (reconciliation, AP, fund administration), where our settled-outcome data is deepest. Bond and E&O come with the safe-harbor certificate.
- **First customer:** one of our two carrier licensees as fronting partner. Buyers are the ~40 agent vendors already in our acceptance protocol.
- **Makes obsolete:** our own data-licensing line to rival carriers (channel conflict, so we pick one partner), certification as a separate product, and questionnaire-based underwriting.

### Thesis B (reserved slot, 10%): Replication clearing for discovery agents
Capability: discovery agents are routine in materials and drug design (EXTRAPOLATION, M) → Adoption: candidate output is far larger than wet-lab capacity → Bottleneck: independent, physical verification (EXTRAPOLATION, M) → **Layer we own:** an outcome-priced replication network. We contract autonomous and CRO lab capacity, keep the data rights, and sell replication certificates that regulators, financiers and insurers accept → Data: claimed versus replicated results by agent and model family → Next capability: a reliability prior that prices royalty financing of agent-discovered assets (SPECULATION, L).
- **Wedge:** replication of battery and catalyst candidates for 2 materials firms. **First customer:** a materials firm whose agent pipeline is backlogged at synthesis.
- **Makes obsolete:** in-house replication teams.

### Playbook lessons applied
- **[E8-forecast] Delayed truth is priced by whoever holds the variance:** Thesis A finally holds it, through fronted capacity rather than float.
- **[E7-forecast] Sell rights, not labor:** we stop selling our loss-relevant data to the parties we would compete with.
- **[E8-forecast] Stacked speculations multiply:** A rests on two premises (safe harbors at M, a large premium pool at M/L), so ~0.35–0.45 jointly. The fallback is to stay a data licensor.
- **[E4] Credit calendar:** a pre-agreed sale trigger for the acceleration branch.
- **Argued against [E8-forecast] "clearing house built by flow-holders":** Thesis B is a clearing house again. We accept the risk only because regulators and financiers, not the labs, need the certificate ([E6] mandate test).
