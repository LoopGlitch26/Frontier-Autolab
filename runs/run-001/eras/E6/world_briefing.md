# World Briefing: Era E6 (as of January 1, 2020)
*Prepared by The Record. Only facts public before this date are included.*

## State of computing
- **Mobile:** Smartphone shipments have plateaued at about 1.4 billion a year. Android and iOS form a stable duopoly. Commercial 5G service launched in South Korea and the US (April 2019) and in parts of Europe and China. Huawei was put on a US export blacklist (May 2019).
- **Cloud:** AWS (revenue run-rate about $35B), Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud dominate rented infrastructure. Alibaba Cloud leads in China. Kubernetes is the container standard. Cloud data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery) and managed ML services (SageMaker, 2017) are spreading.
- **Chips:** Nvidia GPUs (V100, T4) dominate neural-network training. Google rents its TPUs (v3) on its cloud. Start-ups are building AI accelerators: Graphcore, Cerebras (unveiled a wafer-scale chip in August 2019) and Habana (bought by Intel, December 2019). TSMC leads at 7nm. Intel's 10nm has been delayed repeatedly. Apple designs its own phone chips with on-device neural engines.

## Artificial intelligence
- **Vision and speech:** Image classification passed human benchmark levels around 2015. Speech recognition is good enough for mass-market assistants: Amazon said in January 2019 that more than 100 million Alexa devices had been sold. Major clouds sell vision, speech and moderation APIs.
- **Language:** The Transformer architecture (Google, 2017) now underlies the state of the art. Google's BERT (October 2018) raised results on many language benchmarks and is used in Google Search (October 2019). OpenAI's GPT-2 (February 2019, 1.5B parameters) generates fluent paragraphs of text. OpenAI released it in stages over misuse concerns and published the full model in November 2019. Nvidia (Megatron, 8.3B parameters) and Google (T5) trained larger models in 2019. OpenAI's 2018 analysis estimated the compute used in the largest training runs had doubled about every 3.4 months since 2012.
- **Games and science:** DeepMind's AlphaGo (2016) and AlphaZero (2017); AlphaStar reached Grandmaster level in StarCraft II (2019); OpenAI Five beat the Dota 2 world champions (April 2019). DeepMind's AlphaFold placed first in protein-structure prediction at CASP13 (December 2018).
- **Labs and money:** OpenAI became a "capped-profit" company (March 2019) and took a $1B investment from Microsoft (July 2019). Chinese vision firms SenseTime and Megvii were added to a US export blacklist (October 2019).
- **Autonomy:** Waymo launched a limited paid robotaxi service in the Phoenix area (December 2018). An Uber test vehicle killed a pedestrian (March 2018).
- **Concerns:** Synthetic "deepfake" video, bias in face recognition (San Francisco banned city use of it, May 2019), and AI-driven misinformation.

## Platforms and markets
- Facebook has about 2.45 billion monthly users across its main app, plus Instagram (over 1 billion) and WhatsApp. It announced the Libra digital currency (June 2019), which drew regulatory opposition. ByteDance's TikTok/Douyin is growing fast worldwide on algorithmic video feeds. Amazon, Apple, Microsoft and Alphabet are each valued near or above $1 trillion.
- Uber and Lyft went public in 2019 and trade below their IPO prices. WeWork withdrew its IPO (September 2019), and investors are asking tougher questions about unprofitable growth. Zoom, Slack (direct listing), CrowdStrike and Datadog listed in 2019. Stripe was valued at $35B (September 2019).
- **Crypto:** Bitcoin peaked near $20,000 in December 2017 and ended 2019 near $7,000 after the ICO boom and bust.

## Other frontiers
- **Space:** SpaceX reuses Falcon 9 boosters routinely, flew Falcon Heavy (2018), flew an uncrewed Crew Dragon to the ISS (March 2019) and launched its first 120 Starlink satellites.
- **Biology:** CRISPR therapies have entered trials. The He Jiankui germline-editing case (November 2018) prompted global condemnation. mRNA developers (Moderna, IPO December 2018; BioNTech, IPO October 2019) are in clinical trials. A human genome costs roughly $1,000 to sequence.
- **Quantum:** Google claimed "quantum supremacy" on a 53-qubit processor (October 2019). IBM disputed it.
- **Energy:** Lithium-ion pack prices fell to about $156/kWh (BNEF, 2019). Solar and wind are the cheapest new power in many markets.

## Capital climate
- US venture investment was over $130B in both 2018 and 2019. SoftBank's ~$100B Vision Fund wrote mega-rounds. After WeWork, late-stage investors are more cautious. The Fed cut rates three times in 2019 (to 1.50–1.75%). US equities are near record highs. The US–China trade war and tariffs persist, with a "phase one" deal announced in December 2019.

## Society and regulation
- **Privacy:** GDPR has been in force since May 2018. California's CCPA takes effect January 1, 2020. Facebook agreed to a $5B FTC penalty (July 2019) after Cambridge Analytica. Apple's Safari restricts cross-site tracking (Intelligent Tracking Prevention, 2017). Google announced a "Privacy Sandbox" (August 2019).
- **Content and antitrust:** Governments are pressing platforms on harmful content (Germany's NetzDG, the Christchurch Call of May 2019, the UK Online Harms white paper). US antitrust probes of Google, Facebook, Amazon and Apple opened in 2019. The EU fined Google repeatedly.
- **Labor:** California's AB5 (signed September 2019) targets gig-worker classification. Mood: public "techlash" alongside strong markets.
