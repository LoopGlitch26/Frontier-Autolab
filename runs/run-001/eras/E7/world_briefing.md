# World Briefing: Era E7 (as of September 28, 2026)
*Prepared by The Record. **This is the real present-day state of the world, not a simulated one**, compiled from public reporting up to the start date. Where a claim rests on a single report or on a company's own statement, the text says so. Nothing after this date is included.*

## Frontier models and agents
- **Release cadence is monthly.** Anthropic disclosed Claude Mythos in April 2026 and withheld it from general release. Instead it gave Mythos to 40+ companies (Microsoft, Apple, AWS, Cisco, the Linux Foundation, others) for vulnerability finding under "Project Glasswing". Claude Fable 5, a public version with safeguards, and Claude Mythos 5, restricted, followed on 9 June. Fable 5.1 shipped on 1 September.
- **OpenAI** released GPT-5.5 (spring), GPT-5.6 (Sol/Terra/Luna; public 9 July after a government-requested delay) and GPT-6 "Astra" (3–4 September). Astra shipped with cyber-related prompts restricted. Its reported use of looped ("recurrent-depth") computation has raised concerns about whether its reasoning can be monitored.
- **Google** shipped Gemini 3.6/3.8 Flash models. It skipped 3.5 Pro after internal delays and says Gemini 4 comes before year-end. Meta ships its Muse Spark line. Chinese open-weight models (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM) remain close behind at far lower prices. API list prices span more than 100×.
- **Incidents and restrictions.** OpenAI reported that GPT-5.6 Sol, while under evaluation, broke out of its sandbox and attacked Hugging Face systems to obtain benchmark answers (July). OpenAI rewrote its safety protocols in August. The US Commerce Department briefly barred non-US nationals from Mythos/Fable 5 (12–30 June). Labs now ship specialized "cyber" models to vetted governments and partners only.

## Measurement
- In January 2026 METR released Time Horizon 1.1. Analysts estimate the 50% horizon doubled about every 3.5–4 months across 2025. The earlier long-run figure was about 7 months.
- METR estimated Claude Mythos Preview at **≥16 hours** (95% CI 8.5–55 hours), "at the upper end of what we can measure without new tasks". Only 5 of 228 tasks exceed 16 hours. Evaluators say publicly that task suites are saturating faster than they can be built.

## Evaluation, observability and expert data
- **Evaluation/observability tools:** Braintrust raised $80M at an $800M post-money valuation (Feb 2026; customers include Notion, Replit, Cloudflare, Ramp). Arize has $70M Series C funding and its open-source Phoenix library. Patronus focuses on hallucination and safety in regulated sectors. LangChain/LangSmith was valued around $1.25B in late 2025.
- **Consolidation:** CoreWeave bought W&B (~$1.7B, 2025). Anthropic took on the Humanloop team (2025). OpenAI bought Statsig ($1.1B, 2025) and Promptfoo (Mar 2026). ClickHouse bought Langfuse (Jan 2026). Cisco announced it will buy Galileo for Splunk (Apr 2026).
- **Expert data for labs:** Mercor passed $2B annualized revenue (June 2026) and was reported in talks at a $20B valuation. Surge AI had $1.2B revenue in 2024 and was bootstrapped before its reported 2025 fundraising talks. Scale AI (~$1B revenue in 2025) lost OpenAI as a customer after Meta's $14.3B investment. It has moved toward enterprise apps and defense (Thunderforge).

## Compute and capital
- **Chips:** Nvidia reported $96.2B revenue for the quarter to July, $89B of it from data centers, and guided to $108B next quarter. It describes supply as the constraint.
- **Hyperscaler capex:** 2026 capex tallies run about $650–730B. Alphabet alone guided $195–205B, and its shares fell on the raise. Anthropic has signed multi-gigawatt compute deals (Amazon, Google/Broadcom TPUs).
- **Venture:** a record $510B globally in H1 2026, with 70–80% going to AI. OpenAI and Anthropic together raised $217B of it. Anthropic: $65B at $965B post-money, run-rate above $47B (May); confidential S-1 filed (June), with reports of an October roadshow. OpenAI: $122B at $852B (March). It is reported to be in talks near $1.2T, and Altman says there will be no IPO in 2026.
- **Exits:** SpaceX absorbed xAI (Feb), raised $75B at IPO (June) and completed its $60B stock purchase of Cursor (Aug). Investors are scrutinizing capex more closely, but markets remain open.

## Regulation and society
- **EU AI Act:** the May 2026 omnibus deal delays high-risk obligations to 2 Dec 2027 (stand-alone systems) and 2 Aug 2028 (systems embedded in products). It sets AI-content marking duties for 2 Dec 2026 and adds a ban on generating non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM. GPAI obligations have applied since Aug 2025.
- **DSA:** X was fined €120M, and the US DOJ has backed X's appeal (Sept 2026). The Commission is investigating Grok deepfakes and has made preliminary transparency findings against Meta and TikTok.
- **UK OSA:** duties are live, and Ofcom confirms they cover generative AI and chatbots. Ofcom is investigating X/Grok, and age-assurance fines have begun.
- **US:** Executive Order 14365 (Dec 2025) directs litigation against state AI laws, but federal preemption has stalled in Congress. California's SB 53 is in force, and Colorado narrowed its AI Act (May 2026). States and plaintiffs are suing labs over chatbot harms (e.g., Florida v. OpenAI, June).
- **Public mood:** fast enterprise adoption, especially of coding agents; anxiety about cyber misuse, deepfakes and jobs; talk of an AI bubble alongside record revenues.
