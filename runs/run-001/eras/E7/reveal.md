# The Auditor: Audit and Forecast Score, Era E7 (Sep 2026 → Dec 2031)
*Call under review: **Proofworks**. It sells long-horizon, reward-hack-resistant environments built from consented enterprise workflows and deployed-agent failures (Foundry) to neolabs, open-weight labs and a safety institute, then to one frontier lab. The Clearproof evaluation SaaS goes into a sale process (floor ~$400M). The signed ledger becomes an open trace-evidence schema. Agent underwriting (Assure) is a gated option. 10% goes to embodied environments. There is no answer key for this era, so every outcome below is a forecast.*

## 4. Core-bottleneck confidence and kill case
**Confidence that "verified long-horizon tasks are the scarce input" is still real and important at end-2031: ~35%.** It is likely true through 2027 (~75%). After that, three substitutes compete: **(1) RL on production usage** from lab-owned agent products (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini in Workspace); **(2) synthetic environment generation**; **(3) direct enterprise training deals** (Prime Intellect, GDM's own apps).
**Strongest kill case:** the scarce input moves from *environments* to *rights to real deployments*, and those rights go to whoever runs the agent in production. That is the lab or the platform, not a vendor that sold its SaaS funnel. This is [E3] in a new form: the auction owner absorbs the measurement. Carmen's dissent is the kill case.

## 5. Simulated outcome
**Base (~55%):** ~$9M contracted from 3–4 buyers by mid-2027, so criterion #1 passes narrowly. Environment prices fall ~40% by 2028. The SaaS sells in Q1 2027 for ~$350–450M, with trace rights carved out. In 2028 a top lab cuts vendor spend and the in-sourcing tripwire fires, so Foundry pivots to continual certification for AI Act high-risk deployers. Assure binds ~6 policies by Q4 2027: the gate fails and it continues as a data partnership. **2031:** ~$90–130M revenue, worth ~$1–2B, a likely acquisition target. Useful, not era-defining.
**Bear (~30%):** labs in-source by mid-2027 and Deeptune/Mercor undercut on price, so #1 fails. The SaaS bid stalls below $400M in a capex correction. Sold for ~$250–500M in 2028–29, or taken in a license-and-hire deal.
**Bull (~15%):** carriers adopt the open trace schema. Assure becomes the claims-evidence standard for agent cover, the cyber-insurance analog of 2015–2022. Valued at $3–6B.

## 6. Lessons (full text in playbook.md, [E7-forecast])
1. Last era's road not taken is this era's crowded trade.
2. When buyers can build what they buy, sell rights, not labor.
3. Point the tripwire at the likeliest substitute.
4. Don't sell the funnel that produces your moat's raw material.
