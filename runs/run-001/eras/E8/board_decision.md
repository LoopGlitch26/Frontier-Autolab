## 2. DECISION (CEO)

**Name:** **Clearwork** (renamed from Proofworks).
**Identity:** "The neutral clearing house for delegated agent work: we record the authority granted, and we settle what the work was worth when the truth arrives."

**Reinvention thesis:**
- **Capability:** multi-week agents, with reasoning monitoring weakening (FACT(2026) H for looped models; EXT M for work-weeks).
- **Adoption:** outcome-priced contracts are the default, and regulated buyers run agents from several vendors (EXT M; plurality 40% per Ines).
- **Bottleneck:** scope of authority, its revisions and its acceptance can't be proven. Ground truth arrives months after payment, and buyer and vendor dispute it (EXT, M).
- **Layer we own:** mandate-to-settlement clearing. We accept AP2, Entra and OTel formats and take fees in basis points. Funds flow through a licensed escrow partner, and we never hold float or risk.
- **Data:** mandate × revisions × acceptances × consequence at t+30/90/365. Labs can't see it from their own products.
- **Next capability:** carrier-priced warranties and "authority limits" underwritten on settled-outcome history (Assure, with its own data at last). We also license consequence-graded trajectories in regulated domains, the one surviving RL niche (EXT, M-H).

**KILL:**
- Point-in-time certification as our identity. It is headed for checklist prices (EXT, M). We harvest it but **do not sell it** ([E7] "don't sell the funnel"). Certification clients become settlement counterparties.
- Foundry as a standalone environment vendor. Its graders move into consequence scoring.
- The exposure registry as a product. We license anonymized inventory data to one incumbent cat modeler, on fees only.
- Thesis B's operator, as our identity.
- Any balance-sheet insurance risk, and any float.

**KEEP:**
- The signed ledger and our stewardship of the OTel agent-trace schema.
- The monthly re-test pipeline, repurposed as mandate-conformance checks.
- The evaluation-science and reward-hack team.
- Perpetual trace-contribution rights and opt-in pooled results.

**Wedge:** *Clearwork Settle.* Mandate capture, revision log, acceptance sign-off, then escrowed release against delayed outcomes, for outcome-priced reconciliation and software-migration contracts. Pricing: 50–150 bps of settled value plus a per-mandate fee (SPEC, M).

**First customer:** 3 agent vendors selling on outcome price to EU fund administrators and mid-market lenders who reject vendor self-grading (Moffatt, FACT(2026) H). Vendor-side sale keeps it voluntary and multi-buyer ([E2]).

**3-year plan:**
- **2032:** 10 vendors, $300M settled-value run-rate, one escrow partner, one Big-4 acceptance protocol.
- **2033:** $1.5B settled. Consequence data licensed to one lab and one cat modeler.
- **2034:** first carrier-priced warranty on settled data.

**Capital:** ~$320M cash (EXT, M: ~$400M SaaS sale in 2027, less E7-era burn). New-bet burn ~$40M/yr, with the wedge team at 18 or fewer. No raise. 24-month floor against the ~25% bust tail.

**Kill criteria:**
1. ≥8 vendors and ≥$500M settled-value run-rate by 31 Dec 2033.
2. **Absorption tripwire:** a top-2 lab, a hyperscaler or a card/AP2 network ships *cross-vendor* delayed-outcome settlement. If so, within 2 quarters we become a consequence-data licensor, or we sell.
3. **Plurality tripwire:** multi-vendor share among target buyers stays under 30% by 2033 (the Consolidated scenario). If so, license to the platforms.
4. **Hub tripwire:** more than 40% of settled value comes from one buyer or one vendor.
5. **Need test:** fewer than 5% of settled contracts use delayed-outcome clauses by 2033. That would mean no one needs the number.
