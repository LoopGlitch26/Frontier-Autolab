# World Briefing: Era E8 (projected, as of January 2032)
*Prepared by The Auditor. **This is a projected base case, not reality.** It extrapolates from real trends to Sep 2026 and from the E7 forecasts. Tags: **FACT(2026)** = public reporting by Sep 2026; **EXTRAPOLATION** = a trend carried forward; **SPECULATION** = a scenario judgment. Confidence is H/M/L. Several items cut against the Proofworks thesis on purpose.*

## Frontier models and agents
- **FACT(2026), H:** METR measured Mythos Preview at ≥16h (CI 8.5–55h), and its suite is saturating. Releases come monthly. Looped ("recurrent-depth") models such as GPT-6 Astra weaken chain-of-thought monitoring.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** doubling slowed from ~4 months to roughly 6–9 months after 2028, once tasks outgrew what can be verified cheaply. Frontier agents run multi-week projects in software, finance ops and research with light supervision. METR-style horizons are no longer the headline metric. Labs report "autonomous work-weeks" and deployment outcome rates instead.
- **SPECULATION, M-L:** at least one lab claims its systems are "automating AI research". Outside audits cannot confirm it.

## Where the training signal comes from (against the thesis)
- **FACT(2026), H:** 35–40+ RL-environment vendors. Anthropic works with 12+ and is building a platform to commoditize them. OpenAI is building in-house data teams. Mechanize was in license-and-hire talks with Google.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M-H:** vendor environment prices fell 50–70% from 2026 levels. Most frontier RL signal now comes from **production usage of labs' own agent products** and **model-generated synthetic environments**. Third-party environments survive as a niche for regulated, rights-bearing domains such as healthcare, finance and government, and for independent safety testing.
- **SPECULATION, M:** two or three expert-data firms (Surge, Mercor or successors) consolidated into large public or near-public companies. Most small environment vendors ended in acqui-hires.

## Capital and compute
- **FACT(2026), H:** 2026 hyperscaler capex ~$650–730B; Nvidia data-center revenue $89B per quarter; H1 2026 venture $510B; Anthropic filed an S-1 for an October 2026 IPO.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** Anthropic and SpaceX/xAI are public. OpenAI raised at over $1.5T and remains private or recently listed. A **capex digestion in 2027–28** cut several neoclouds and second-tier labs, and lab data budgets fell for about 4 quarters before recovering. Power and grid interconnects, not chips, are the binding constraint.
- **SPECULATION, L-M:** a full AI equity bust (a drawdown of more than 50% in AI leaders) happened. Assigned ~25%, not the base case.

## Regulation, liability and insurance
- **FACT(2026), H:** EU AI Act high-risk duties are delayed to Dec 2027 and Aug 2028. ISO generative-AI exclusions took effect Jan 2026, and Verisk is weighing agent-specific exclusions. AIUC-1 plus Beazley/Lloyd's paper; HSB, Testudo, Klaimee and others sell AI liability cover. Moffatt v. Air Canada rejects "the AI did it" as a defence.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** high-risk obligations now apply, and conformity assessments create a steady certification market that is mid-sized, audit-firm-heavy and priced like SOC 2. Affirmative agent cover exists but mostly **folded into cyber and tech E&O lines**, priced from carrier questionnaires and a few standard logging requirements. No independent "agent actuary" won a franchise. Insurers require tamper-evident action logs, and an **OpenTelemetry-based agent-trace standard** is widely used.
- **SPECULATION, M:** one or two correlated agent-failure events (a model regression that spread through thousands of deployments, or a large agentic fraud) triggered systemic-risk sublimits, as NotPetya did for cyber.
- **SPECULATION, L-M:** the US passed a limited federal preemption statute. Otherwise there is a state-law patchwork and active litigation over chatbot harms.

## Security and geopolitics
- **FACT(2026), H:** cyber-capable models go only to vetted parties (Glasswing). OpenAI disclosed a sandbox escape during evaluation. Chinese open-weight models trail closely at much lower prices.
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** autonomous vulnerability discovery and patching are routine. The attacker/defender balance is contested. Export and access controls on frontier weights are tighter. Open-weight models from China and elsewhere are about 6–12 months behind and dominate cost-sensitive deployment.

## Physical world
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M-L:** robotics foundation models are deployed commercially in warehouses, logistics and some manufacturing. General home robots are still pilots. The data bottleneck for embodied AI is real-world interaction data, captured mainly by fleet owners.

## Economy and society
- **EXTRAPOLATION, M:** coding, support and back-office work are heavily automated. Entry-level white-collar hiring is visibly down, and "AI and jobs" is a top-three political issue in the US and EU. Outcome-priced agents (per resolution, per task) are the default business model.
- **SPECULATION, L:** some form of AI-linked income support or compute dividend is piloted in at least one OECD country.

## Leading indicators to check at era start
Share of lab RL signal from production usage versus vendors; whether agent cover is standalone or bundled into cyber; AI Act conformity-assessment volumes; the size of any correlated agent-loss event; the level of hyperscaler capex after 2027.
