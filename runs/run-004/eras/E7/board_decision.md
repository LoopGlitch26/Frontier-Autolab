# E7 board decision — September 2026

## Decision

**Company:** Caseground — independent acceptance testing for one dispute-operations workflow.

**Reinvention thesis:** Existing agent and payment products can assemble dispute evidence, yet a buyer still needs to know whether a particular workflow achieves its reliability target at an acceptable level of human review and cost. The capability jump is agentic workflow execution; adoption is constrained by demonstrable performance, authority, and operating burden. The layer to test is workflow-specific qualification, using permissioned cases and buyer-defined material-error criteria. Keep all decisions and submissions with the customer.

**Wedge:** A paid, manual evaluation of one merchant's existing dispute-evidence tool on a permissioned historical sample. Compare baseline workflow and tool-assisted workflow for packet completeness, material error, reviewer intervention/time, and cost. No generic agent platform or integration product.

**Three-year plan:** Secure one paid pilot and data approval; preregister the case rubric and oversight policy; evaluate on a held-out sample; deliver an auditable report. Seek a second payer with the same workflow before building reusable tooling. Stop if results do not affect a real procurement/deployment decision.

**Kill criteria:** No budget owner/data permission; no paid pilot; inability to define material error; evaluation doesn't change workflow or procurement; review/security cost exceeds the value; or second buyer requires >20% bespoke work.

**Kill / keep:** Kill the evidence-packet product build: Stripe and Chargeflow already offer evidence assembly/submission capabilities. Keep case taxonomy, permissioned data protocol, and human-review measurement. The pivot to qualification remains a speculative commercial thesis, not a validated market.

**Dissent:** CEO argues an independent workflow result is a distinct buyer outcome. CTO warns READY and platform QA may already suffice. CFO authorizes only a paid test, no staffing or broad platform development. Red Team's interview and second-buyer gates stand.
