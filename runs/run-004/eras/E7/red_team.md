# Independent Red Team critique — E7 · September 2026

The studies inform evaluation design, not demand. IBM's sample does not establish payment-dispute prevalence. READY's 16 systems/750 cases concern clinical audit, not merchant disputes. Microsoft maturity guidance is a framework, not commercial validation. [IBM CAP](https://research.ibm.com/publications/characterizing-agents-in-production) · [Scale READY](https://labs.scale.com/papers/reliable-enterprise-agent-deployment) · [Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/agents/adoption-maturity-model/)

## Frontier — workflow-specific reliability qualification
**Kill case:** “Qualification” may be an internal QA task, and READY/general evaluation vendors may substitute. Evidence packet is also crowded by processor products. Studies do not establish merchant willingness to pay.
**Suggested gates:** Stop if 20 interviews yield <3 with a quantified recurring cost and named budget holder; no paid pilot; <20% net handling-cost reduction after oversight; or second buyer requires >20% customization.
**Cheapest falsification:** Manually evaluate customer-approved historical cases, measure completeness, material error, reviewer time, correction, and delivery/security cost; seek payment, then repeat with second buyer.

## Product — workflow qualification kit
**Kill case:** Another dashboard may add review without changing procurement; independent test-set ownership/data access may be blocked. Do not imply evaluation gives authority to decide/submit.
**Suggested gates:** Blinded 100-case trial; reviewers can verify ≥95% of reported items, material errors ≤2%, and net review time improves ≥20%; second paid buyer ≤20% customization and variable cost <50% revenue.
**Cheapest falsification:** Spreadsheet/manual report on 100 cases before software; measure how buyer uses results.

## Market — merchant vs provider
**Kill case:** Merchant may lack budget, provider may lack data rights or incentives; incumbent functionality could be sufficient.
**Suggested gates:** 10 qualified conversations/channel; continue only if 3 identify approver, approved data path, and accept priced pilot; require paid pilot and paid repeat buyer. Kill if security/custom integration erases margin.

**FACT vs inference:** Published study results and vendor product docs are facts about those sources. Applying them to merchant workflows is extrapolation. READY published Sep 4, 2026, before this briefing date; no hindsight leakage.
