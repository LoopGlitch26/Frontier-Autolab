# Market & Capital memo — E1

**Carmen Ortiz:** A support manager or data-processing manager could sponsor an installation if repeated incidents consume staff time. This is a buyer hypothesis, not confirmed demand. Interview named teams and seek a paid, bounded pilot.

**Arjun Mehta:** $1.5M supports focused software and a small field effort; it does not fund bespoke integration across heterogeneous sites. Preserve runway by shadowing one team before productizing.

**Elise Laurent:** The economic value, if present, is reduced downtime and technician labor. A private linked library may be easy to trial but has unclear recurring revenue and content maintenance cost.

**Recommendation:** Compare two candidate wedges: (A) LAN diagnostics/case records sold to an administrator or support manager; (B) a licensed specialist reference library sold to one technical organization. Require baseline labor and a named budget owner before committing to either. Price a pilot against measured support time saved; stop if the service burden is bespoke.
