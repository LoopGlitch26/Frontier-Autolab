# Frontier Research memo — E6 · January 2020

Transformer and language-model research, cloud APIs, and production computer vision are technical context, not demand. The department recommends keeping a one-category payment-dispute evidence packet, with an authorized human reviewer and no model decision.

1. **Completeness checker:** permissioned checklist for required evidence; test missing-item detection separately from material-error rate. Later suggest gaps only with reviewer oversight. Risk: rules vary and sensitive data costs.
2. **Returns evidence review:** manual checklist for one disputed return; measure completeness, errors, review cost, permission, and second payer. Risk: policies/data differ.

**Yuki** says no market is proven; **Samuel** rejects model/API novelty as proof; **Ines** sees narrow advisory help; **Kofi** requires separate completeness, material error, correction time, and security measures. Playbook consequence: evidence assembly is not decision authority; count access/security costs and require another payer.
