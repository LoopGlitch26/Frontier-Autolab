# E6 world briefing — 1 January 2020

**Information cutoff:** 1 January 2020. Events later in 2020 are excluded.

Cloud APIs and SaaS are mature delivery approaches. Transformer-based language-model research is public; BERT and GPT-2 have demonstrated notable language capabilities, while questions of reliability, evaluation, and useful deployment remain open. Computer vision is used in production settings, but performance remains sensitive to data, operating conditions, and error costs. GDPR is in force, and privacy/security obligations are central when handling payment and customer evidence.

Switchyard Evidence Operations is testing permissioned evidence preparation for one payment-dispute case type. No durable market, software margin, or second-customer repeatability is established. A consequential reviewer retains authority. The company must distinguish a research capability from a reliable workflow and price security, review, and correction into delivery costs.
