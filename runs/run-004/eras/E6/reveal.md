# E6 historical reveal and score — hindsight review

The following pandemic-era and later language-model outcomes are deliberately excluded from the Jan 2020 decision assessment. At the cutoff, model research was promising but operational reliability, buyer demand, and exact dispute workflows were unknown. The board's measurement distinction—completeness versus material error—was sound. Its main weakness is choosing a narrow check that may be absorbed into existing payment, case-management, or merchant platforms.

Base scenario: a rules-based tool earns a small deployment if evidence rules stabilize and it measurably reduces reviewer effort. Bear scenario: rule maintenance/security work exceeds saved labor, or existing processors bundle the function. Neither is an observed outcome.

## Playbook update

1. Evaluate omission detection and material error as different metrics.
2. Shadow-mode assistance is safer than consequential automation but still needs value evidence.
3. Existing platforms can absorb a useful feature; identify distribution and system-of-record control.
4. Measure oversight and security costs as part of the product, not compliance overhead.
