# Product & Engineering memo — E6 · January 2020

**Priya:** Improve packet quality within one case type. **Jonah:** measure missing and materially wrong evidence; speed alone is inadequate. **Hana:** only named humans approve/submit; implement permission, retention, correction, and deletion. **Leo:** one connector at most after a second buyer; BERT/GPT-2/CV are not validation.

1. **Dispute packet completeness checker:** controlled import, checklist gaps/inconsistencies, reviewer approval. Risks: variable rules, wrong extracted fields, false confidence, connector/security cost.
2. **Evidence review/correction log:** source, reviewer state, correction, approval, export. Risks: duplicate records, staff burden, access/audit cost.

Use explicit rules and human review first. Require second paying customer with same category and requirements. Apply the Playbook: distinguish completeness from error, evidence from decision authority, and include data costs.
