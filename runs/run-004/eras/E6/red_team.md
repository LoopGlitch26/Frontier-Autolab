# Independent Red Team critique — E6 · January 2020

Gates are proposed, not evidence. A complete packet can still contain misleading material; keep human decisions/submissions.

## Frontier / product — completeness checker
**Kill case:** Buyer and cost of incomplete packets unknown; rules vary and sensitive records are costly. Completeness does not establish correctness.
**Gate:** One paid pilot/data owner; on 100 historical packets catch ≥95% missing items with <5% false alerts; measure material error separately. Product must reduce net reviewer time ≥20%, have zero unauthorized access, and a second payer with ≤20% customization.
**Cheap test:** Manual checklist on historical packets, independent reviewer scoring, measure time, security needs, and paid-pilot commitment.

## Market — processor budget
**Kill case:** Processor may see volume without budget, data rights, or authority. Merchant operations has same unresolved issues.
**Gate:** Of 10 qualified buyers in each channel, 3 identify costly recurring problem, approver/data authority and paid-pilot path; require first and second paid buyer with ≤20% customization; count human/security cost.
**Cheap test:** Offer the same priced manual checker pilot to processors and merchants using approved data; require payment and verified authority.

No pandemic or later outcomes are used.
