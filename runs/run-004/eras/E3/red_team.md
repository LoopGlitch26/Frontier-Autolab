# Independent Red Team critique — E3 · January 2002

Thresholds below are proposed experiment gates, not observed evidence.

## Frontier — e-commerce discrepancy triage

**Kill case:** The buyer, unresolved-case cost, and authority to change workflow are unspecified. Varying order systems can make the product custom integration; faster sorting is not enough if resolution does not improve.

**Suggested gates:** Identify a budget owner and paid pilot. On at least 50 historical cases, correctly prioritize 80% and reduce median time to next action 20% without increased misrouting. Stop if data is unreliable or rules differ materially by prospect.

**Cheapest falsification:** Manually classify one buyer's past discrepancies and compare with actual handling; seek paid commitment before connectors.

## Product — guided diagnostic workbench

**Kill case:** Correctness depends on current procedures and configuration. Interface work before procedure validation risks automating bad advice; an editor merely shifts upkeep to users.

**Suggested gates:** On 30 repeat incidents, achieve 80% correct next steps, 15% faster median handling, and expert correction below 20%; require a funded pilot and named content owner.

**Cheapest falsification:** Paper/database decision tree scored by an expert; separately test procedure updates.

## Market — IT support vs provider

**Kill case:** Internal IT may lack budget authority; providers may face diverse environments and new support burden. User interest is not a purchase. Repeatability is unproven.

**Suggested gates:** Interview 10 qualified buyers in each channel; require 3 credible budget paths in the chosen channel and one signed paid pilot. For a second customer, require the core workflow to work with at most 20% customer-specific implementation effort.

**Cheapest falsification:** Offer both segments the same priced, manually supported pilot and record who supplies cases, uses the workflow, maintains content, and pays. Kill both if neither commits.

**Date check:** Do not infer market size from later e-commerce growth. Validate case volume, cost, and budget as of January 2002.
