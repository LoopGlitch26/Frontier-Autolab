# Product & Engineering memo — E3 · January 2002

**Priya:** Retain a local mode; broadband is uneven. **Jonah:** distinguish technician from service manager and budget owner. **Hana:** XML/Web services are implementation options, not reasons to send incident data to a hosted service. **Leo:** portable data and customer-authored procedure formats may avoid lock-in but could create maintenance risk.

## Proposals

1. **Guided diagnostic workbench:** structured checks and case summaries for the existing supported PC/LAN setup. Test draft decision paths manually against repeat incidents; only later use reviewed outcomes to suggest next checks. Risks: extra entry, stale procedures, and scope expansion.
2. **Customer-maintained procedure editor:** local editor for a support lead to update and export tested procedures. Wedge: replace printed checklists for one recurring issue class. Later consider sharing only if requested. Risks: procedures may rarely change and customers may not maintain accuracy.

The Playbook requires a manual prototype, separate user/buyer/budget validation, and predeclared kill gates. Platform activity is not demand. Broadband support, XML and Web services remain hypotheses, not product requirements.
