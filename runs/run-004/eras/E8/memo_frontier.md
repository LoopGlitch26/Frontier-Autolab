# Frontier Research memo — projected January 2032

This is a forecast, not a report. FACT as of 2026: IBM CAP and Scale READY provide evidence that human review, workflow reliability, and cost matter in current samples/frameworks. **Yuki** favors proving one paid workflow; **Samuel** cautions these signals do not prove 2032 demand; **Ines** sees possible external acceptance testing; **Kofi** says buyer-owned targets and authorized cases are prerequisites.

1. **Independent dispute-agent acceptance tests:** compare an agent to buyer-defined completeness, material-error, escalation, oversight, and cost thresholds. Risk: platform-native QA or internal testing suffices.
2. **Permission-boundary tests:** test whether a workflow accesses permitted records and stops before reserved customer actions. Risk: identity/control vendors already enforce this and customer policies may be unclear.

Both bets are speculative. Require payment and second-buyer reuse. Counterforces: native evaluations, data restrictions, manual processes. Apply Playbook: no productization absent repeat paid use and total oversight costs.
