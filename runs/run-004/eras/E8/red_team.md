# Independent Red Team critique — projected January 2032

All claims are projections. Native evaluation, data rights, and manual operations could remove the need for an independent product.

## Frontier — independent acceptance tests
**Kill case:** Buyers may trust platform tests or lack authorized case data; reports may not alter decisions.
**Gate:** Stop without payment, authorized test cases, material findings beyond existing QA, or second buyer reusing ≥80% of test method.
**Cheap test:** Fixed-price blinded test on approved historical cases; compare with internal evaluation; measure effort and effect on deployment decision.

## Product — service/portal or assurance layer
**Kill case:** Consultancy labor undermines margins; assurance duplicates native platform tools.
**Gate:** Setup ≤3 person-days, expert review ≤2 person-days, ≥50% gross margin after labor, second paid deployment ≤20% custom work; otherwise stop.
**Cheap test:** Manual delivery, cost every hour, compare with native test; ask second buyer to purchase same protocol.

## Market — merchant vs PSP
**Kill case:** Merchant may lack evaluation budget; PSP may lack rights/incentives. Channel size is not evidence.
**Gate:** Interview 10 per channel; ≥3 identify budget holder, data approval, priced pilot; require paid first/second buyer with ≥80% reusable assets.
**Cheap test:** Same priced acceptance test offered to both; require payment, permission, and evidence findings inform decision.

No projected condition is established as fact.
