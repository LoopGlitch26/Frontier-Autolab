# Frontier Research memo — E4 · January 2008

**Yuki:** The 50-case gate and second customer are real tests, not formalities. **Samuel:** SaaS/cloud and mobile are infrastructure signals, not proof of Switchyard demand. **Ines:** A hosted service or inventory exception workflow may become possible, but neither is validated. **Kofi:** Track case mix, time, handoffs, authority, resolution, and service labor; manual workflow does not demonstrate software margin.

1. **Repeat merchant exception triage:** manually classify one recurring exception type; learn whether one authorized action owner and case taxonomy repeat across two merchants. Risk: bespoke rules and review labor.
2. **Inventory discrepancy review:** manually compare one merchant's stated inventory and fulfillable stock; record correction owner/time. Risk: inconsistent records and low frequency.

No connectors until buyer and second-customer gates pass. The Playbook requires authorized next action and repeatability; cloud availability is not demand.
