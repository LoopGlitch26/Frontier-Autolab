# E4 world briefing — 1 January 2008

**Information cutoff:** 1 January 2008. Do not use later events in the 2008 financial crisis.

Web commerce and social platforms are established parts of the consumer Internet, while SaaS adoption and cloud infrastructure are growing. Amazon Web Services launched in 2006; Salesforce and other hosted software businesses have demonstrated subscription delivery. The iPhone shipped in 2007, though smartphone use and mobile workflows are early. Businesses still operate mixed on-premise and hosted systems; integrations, identity, and data quality remain practical constraints.

Switchyard Commerce Operations has only a manual exception-triage hypothesis from one merchant. It has no confirmed paying customer, no proven authority to change the outcome of a case, and no evidence that a service can become repeatable software. Its next move must distinguish operational value from the economics of software and must fit small-team capital. All adoption and buyer claims remain hypotheses.
