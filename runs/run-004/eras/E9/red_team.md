# Independent Red Team critique — projected January 2040

This is a conditional test of the E8 thesis, not a market conclusion. Buyer demand, platform functions, and data rights in 2040 are speculative.

## Frontier — decision-grade test vs stress test
**Kill case:** Independent testing matters only if it informs a real deployment/renewal decision and is trusted over native QA. Stress tests may generate noise; no data rights or action authority means an expensive report.
**Gate:** Stop without paid evaluation tied to named decision and authorized cases; in 1 of 3 pilots, findings must change deployment/remediation/renewal.
**Cheap test:** Fixed-price manual evaluation, pre-agreed criteria, compare findings to native QA and record decision change.

## Product — independent evaluation tool
**Kill case:** Software could automate low-value reporting; expert review, data handling, and implementation consume margins.
**Gate:** Three paid evaluations before productization; ≥80% reusable protocol/artifacts, ≤20% customization; expert labor <50% revenue; record changed decisions.
**Cheap test:** Same manual evaluation with two buyers; compare cost/usefulness with internal/native QA.

## Market — merchant vs PSP
**Kill case:** Merchant may lack QA budget; PSP may lack incentive/authority. Native/internal QA may be adequate.
**Gate:** Of 10 qualified buyers/channel, ≥3 identify approver, data path, accept priced pilot; at least 1/3 pilots changes a decision; repeat buyer and ≤20% customization.
**Cheap test:** Fixed-price offer plus approved data, deliver manually, compare with own QA.

All 2040 conditions remain speculation; proceed only on payment, permission, incremental findings, decision impact, and viable repeat delivery.
