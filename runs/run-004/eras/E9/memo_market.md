# Market & Capital memo — projected January 2040

All 2040 market statements are conditional projections. There is no evidence E8's proposed test succeeded or produced repeat revenue.

1. **Merchant dispute operations:** may pay only if a packet check finds material gaps or reduces review effort beyond native QA; budget owner must use result and retain action authority. Counterforce: native tools and no data access.
2. **Provider/acquirer support:** may pay if same workflow repeats across merchants; counterforce: different permissions, no budget control, platform bundling.

Require buyer-defined target, actual use in a decision, native-QA comparison, second paying buyer, and all human/security/data costs in margin. Payment alone is not proof of scale; no TAM claim. Playbook: no staffing or broad integration until repeat contribution margin is measured.
