# Product & Engineering memo — E5 · January 2014

**Priya:** A 16-person team can build a focused SaaS workbench but must retain human authority. **Jonah:** identify user, payer, and authorized operator separately. **Hana:** tenant separation, access, audit, retention, and deletion matter. **Leo:** one import/export path; no connector without repeat demand; ML progress is not a product requirement.

1. **Permissioned exception workbench:** one exception type, role assignments, evidence, correction history, and explicit authorized action. Next capability: common connector requested by two paying customers. Risks: manual intake and cloud duties.
2. **Reviewed case-resolution assistant:** surface prior cases/checklist; operator edits or rejects and records correction. Risks: sparse/noisy data, misplaced trust, review labor.

Validate a second buyer, authority and workflow first. Require human review and measure acceptance, correction, and resolution time. Apply the Playbook: workflow validation is not margin proof; count review costs and data obligations.
