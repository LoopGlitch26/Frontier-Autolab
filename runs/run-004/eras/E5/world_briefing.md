# E5 world briefing — 1 January 2014

**Information cutoff:** 1 January 2014.

Smartphones are broadly used and mobile commerce is growing. Cloud infrastructure, SaaS, and APIs are now common ways to deliver business software, though security, privacy, integration, and data quality remain concerns. Deep-learning results have drawn renewed attention since 2012, but turning research progress into reliable products and defensible businesses remains uncertain. Online marketplaces and payment services have expanded, creating high-volume digital workflows and new forms of operational risk.

Switchyard's 2008 decision authorized only manual, permissioned exception triage. The company has no validated software margin or proof that a second merchant repeats the same workflow. Any new proposal must show an identifiable buyer and action authority, include human review and data obligations in its costs, and distinguish platform capability from customer demand.
