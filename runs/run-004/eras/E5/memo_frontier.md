# Frontier Research memo — E5 · January 2014

Smartphones, SaaS, APIs, marketplaces, and mobile commerce enable connected workflows, but none proves product demand. **Yuki** insists on full labor accounting; **Samuel** says platform signals must not replace buyer evidence; **Ines** proposes testing returns/dispute evidence work; **Kofi** requires explicit authority, permissioned records, and second-buyer integration measurement.

1. **Cross-channel permissioned exceptions:** standardize taxonomy/permissions across two merchants, measure human review and corrections, then automate only common steps. Risk: every channel differs.
2. **Returns/payment-dispute evidence preparation:** manually assemble an authorized case packet for one case type, measuring completeness, errors, labor, and who can approve/submission. Risk: case rules differ and review erases savings.

Both are hypotheses. Do not ingest unnecessary data or build connectors until two buyers repeat the same workflow. **Playbook:** count all human labor, define action permissions, and require paid repeatability.
