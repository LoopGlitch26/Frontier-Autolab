# E5 historical reveal and score — hindsight review

By later years, digital payments, fraud operations, and transaction-risk vendors expanded substantially. That history does not show this specific evidence-packet business would win. In 2014, mobile/SaaS delivery and deep-learning research were available signals, but the board wisely avoided assuming those signals could reliably decide disputes. The enduring questions are data rights, evidence quality, payment authority, and who bears the cost of a wrong outcome.

Base scenario: a small evidence workflow product sells to a narrow operations team if case rules repeat and security review is manageable. Bear scenario: payment platforms and merchants retain the workflow, rules vary, and manual review outweighs revenue. These outcomes are illustrative.

## Playbook update

1. For consequential records, measure completeness and material error, not only speed.
2. Separate evidence preparation from the authority to decide or submit.
3. Price security and access controls into unit economics.
4. A second paying customer is stronger evidence than technical API availability.
