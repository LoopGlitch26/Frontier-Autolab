# Independent Red Team critique — E5 · January 2014

Gates here are proposed thresholds, not observed facts.

## Frontier — dispute evidence preparation
**Kill case:** Buyer, submission authority, record access, and labor savings unproven; returns and disputes may have different rules.
**Gate:** Paid buyer and permissioned sample; on 50 cases, ≥90% complete packets, <5% material error, ≥20% net handling-time reduction. **Cheap test:** manually prepare real cases under buyer review and ask budget owner to fund continuation.

## Product — permissioned SaaS workbench
**Kill case:** Access controls create real security/retention/support duties; routing alone is not value; review may erase margin.
**Gate:** Paid pilot with named data owner and written scope; authorized use, review <15 minutes/case, variable cost <50% revenue; second paying buyer, ≤20% customization. **Cheap test:** permissioned manual prototype, access-boundary check, labor measure, second paid commitment.

## Market — service-provider buyer
**Kill case:** Provider may lack budget, data rights, and action authority; each merchant's permissions/evidence rules may differ.
**Gate:** Interview 10 providers and 10 merchants; in a channel ≥3 identify budget, rights, and concrete paid-pilot path; require one paid pilot and a second paying buyer ≤20% customization. Count all delivery labor.

No post-2014 outcomes justify the decision.
