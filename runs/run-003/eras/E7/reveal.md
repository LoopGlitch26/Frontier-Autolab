# E7 audit and score — September 2026

Current sources indicate the reliability problem and vendor activity are real, but they do not prove an independent control vendor can win. IBM Research’s production-agent study reports 68% of observed agents execute at most 10 steps before human intervention, 74% rely primarily on human evaluation, and 70% use prompted off-the-shelf models. Microsoft’s maturity model includes evaluation, observability, approvals, rollback, and deployment controls. Scale’s READY framework evaluates agents for workflow deployment; Gartner identifies reliability as an enterprise challenge. These findings support a workflow-specific acceptance wedge and also show competition and platform absorption risk.
