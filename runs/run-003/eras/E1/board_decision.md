# E1 board decision — Jan 1990

**Porthole Networks** will sell diagnostic software and protocol adapters to midsize firms operating mixed PC and LAN environments. Thesis: LAN adoption increases the number of connected endpoints → admins face opaque failures → a portable diagnostics console becomes the wedge → incident and topology data improve troubleshooting → the company can become the network operations console.

Build a DOS/early workstation-compatible probe and service toolkit with a 12-month sales target of 20 regional IT providers. Capital: $1.5M seed. Kill if two vendors provide equivalent cross-network diagnostics or customer support consumes more than half of revenue. Keep network troubleshooting expertise; kill consumer dial-up aspirations. The CTO favors a narrower hardware test instrument; the CFO dissents that adapters are capital-light but easily copied. CEO chooses software plus field services to learn customer topology.
