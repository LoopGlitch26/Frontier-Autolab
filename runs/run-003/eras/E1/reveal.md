# E1 reveal and score — 1990–1995

Office networking and the Internet expanded, while TCP/IP and commercial Internet access gained ground. Networking vendors, ISPs, and operating-system vendors captured much of the durable value; diagnostics and integration remained useful but fragmented. A small Porthole could plausibly become a regional systems tool or be acquired, but its adapters would lose pricing power as standards and bundled management improved.

**Scores:** frontier 7, timing 6, layer 5, courage 6, hindsight protection 6; total **60/100**. The call understands the pain of heterogeneous networks but underweights the value in access and standardized infrastructure. Hindsight score is cautious because the narrative knows which protocols prevailed. **Lesson [E1]:** sell an observable operational improvement now; treat adapters as a wedge, not a moat.
