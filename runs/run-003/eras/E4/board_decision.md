# E4 board decision — Jan 2008

**ClaimGraph** pivots to verified digital evidence and reconciliation for motor claims. Thesis: digital photos and online claim filing increase evidence volume → adjusters face inconsistent documents and duplicate submissions → provenance and reconciliation are bottlenecks → sell into the insurer’s adjudication workflow → labeled exceptions improve triage and fraud detection.

Wedge: document intake and provenance for one regional insurer plus repair network; $4M capital for integrations and security. Kill if no insurer agrees to a paid pilot or if adjusters must rekey every field. Keep workflow design, secure data handling, and integration discipline; stop selling clinic scheduling. The Red Team says carriers will build workflow internally and repair shops lack leverage. CEO chooses the insurer as buyer and refuses a two-sided marketplace.
