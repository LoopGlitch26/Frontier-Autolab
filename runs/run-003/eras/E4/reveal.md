# E4 reveal and score — 2008–2013

Smartphones and cloud software changed how firms captured and processed information. The iPhone launched in 2007; later app ecosystems expanded quickly. Insurers modernized claims operations, but incumbent core systems, adjuster networks, and customer acquisition constrained entrants. ClaimGraph’s evidence layer is plausible, but digital documents by themselves do not create a defensible network. A focused vendor could win through integrations and a distribution partner.

**Scores:** frontier 8, timing 7, layer 6, courage 8, hindsight protection 5; total **65/100**. It pivots decisively toward a payer but still risks being an integration feature. **Lesson [E4]:** digitizing evidence does not create a network; attach to a payer’s adjudication and preserve chain of custody.
