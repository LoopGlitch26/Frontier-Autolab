# E3 reveal and score — 2002–2007

Web applications and hosted software gained legitimacy, while broadband and online services grew. Salesforce demonstrated the viability of subscription enterprise software; Google Analytics later made basic web measurement free. Healthcare technology remained fragmented and regulated, leaving room for workflow vendors but high integration and sales costs. A specialty-focused Clinisphere might have survived as a regional SaaS vendor; expansion across clinics would be slower than its product roadmap suggests.

**Scores:** frontier 7, timing 7, layer 6, courage 7, hindsight protection 5; total **63/100**. The call is economically grounded and narrowly scoped, but its data-access and procurement assumptions are optimistic. **Lesson [E3]:** in regulated workflows, implementation and permissioned data are the product; count repeatable deployment before counting addressable market.
