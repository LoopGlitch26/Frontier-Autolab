# E6 board decision — Jan 2020

**LineSight** becomes a deployment-specific inspection quality company. Thesis: computer vision becomes usable on more industrial lines → factories want automated defect detection → sparse failures, changing lighting, and costly false rejects are bottlenecks → sell validation, drift alarms, and human review around a narrow production line → labeled exceptions improve inspection models and operating decisions.

Wedge: one defect type at one manufacturing site; $8M capital for data collection, edge deployment, and field engineering. Kill if the customer cannot provide representative failure examples or if false rejects erase labor savings. Keep evidence and workflow expertise; stop building benefits software. The scientist argues for a general model API; Red Team says cloud vendors will commoditize inference. CEO sells an acceptance outcome with a human fallback. Hindsight risk is elevated because the later AI boom is known.
