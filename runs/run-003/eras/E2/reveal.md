# E2 reveal and score — 1996–2001

The web grew rapidly, and companies invested in hosting, connectivity, security, and measurement. Monitoring became useful as online services became commercial, but probes and alerts were replicable features of hosting and network products. The dot-com boom and bust made customer survival and cash discipline central. PageSignal could have become a niche monitoring vendor, but the thesis does not own the hosting relationship or remediation.

**Scores:** frontier 6, timing 7, layer 4, courage 6, hindsight protection 6; total **58/100**. Timing is attractive; ownership is weak. **Lesson [E2]:** charge for continuity and resolution, not raw telemetry that an infrastructure vendor can bundle. A hindsight penalty remains because “web operations” is easy to narrate after the category exists.
