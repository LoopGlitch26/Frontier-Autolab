# E9 board decision — January 2040 (forecast)

**Delegation Warranty** arranges capped recourse for one class of software-delegated purchasing transaction, through a licensed insurer or existing balance-sheet partner. **EXTRAPOLATION (low-to-medium):** repeated task-level loss records may improve underwriting. **SPECULATION (low):** contracts and regulation will recognize a separable agent-delegation risk class.

Do not become a carrier. First product: evidence-backed service-level credit with a small defined cap. Keep WorkPermit authority records and claims exception data; stop selling stand-alone permission software if platform APIs suffice. Kill if claim frequency cannot be estimated, legal responsibility is unclear, or buyers will not pay for recourse. The Red Team’s dissent is that enterprise indemnities and existing E&O policies already cover the losses. CEO funds only a broker-led pilot after a partner commits capacity.
